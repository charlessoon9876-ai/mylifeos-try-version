# Book 1 — My Life Origin
## Editorial Upgrade V2

### Why this pass exists
Reader feedback identified repeated ideas across several chapters, especially:

- Chapters 1–2: work / family / health / money all existing at the same time, and the need for a system.
- Chapters 8–10: books explaining only part of life, the search for a complete framework, and the need to connect life domains.
- Repeated chapter openings built around similar statements such as “I started recording”, “I later discovered”, and “life is connected”.

V2 keeps the same 12-chapter architecture but gives every chapter one primary editorial job.

### Chapter jobs

- Prologue — Why the record began.
- Chapter 1 — Who Charles is: observer, problem-solver, systems instinct.
- Chapter 2 — The fragmented tool environment before My Life OS.
- Chapter 3 — ChatGPT moves from utility to mirror.
- Chapter 4 — Two GPT accounts reveal the difference between classification and fragmentation.
- Chapter 5 — Daily Log creates usable life data.
- Chapter 6 — Review turns facts into understanding.
- Chapter 7 — Repeated conditions reveal patterns.
- Chapter 8 — Charles stops searching for isolated answers and starts searching for structure.
- Chapter 9 — Different books / systems have different scopes; the “mountain” idea is kept here only.
- Chapter 10 — Six life domains become the first whole-life map.
- Chapter 11 — My Life OS becomes a feedback loop: Raw Data → Review → Pattern → Decision → Action → New Data.
- Chapter 12 — The repeatable element is the process, not Charles’s personal answers.
- Epilogue — Origin marks the beginning of an evolving system.

### Editorial rules applied

1. One major idea gets one main home.
2. Later chapters must advance the argument rather than re-explain earlier chapters.
3. Real usage moments are preferred over abstract explanation.
4. Chapter openings vary in rhythm and function.
5. Lists are used only when they clarify structure.
6. Chinese remains the editorial master.
7. English and Bahasa Melayu follow the V2 Chinese master.
8. V1 remains preserved in Git history; V2 is implemented as a clean override layer.

### What was deliberately preserved

- 12-chapter structure + Prologue + Epilogue.
- Two GPT account story.
- Daily / Weekly / Monthly Review.
- Six life domains.
- Raw Data → Review → Pattern → Decision logic.
- Charles 1.0 as the first long-term case.
- My Life OS as self-management / awareness, not a guarantee or professional advice.

### Reader test after merge
Ask a reader to read V2 without being told exactly what changed, then answer:

1. Where does the book still feel repetitive?
2. Which chapter most strongly pulls you into the next chapter?
3. Which section sounds most like Charles?
4. Where did your attention drop?

This feedback becomes the input for the next editorial cycle.
